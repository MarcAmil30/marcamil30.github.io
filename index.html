I build models that read and write DNA. I'm a PhD student with Dr Aleksej Zelezniak at King's College London, working on generative models for de novo DNA design and on using genomic language models to predict and control gene expression. Before that: neurotechnology at Imperial, computational protein engineering at UCL, and biosensor hardware at Nutrix.

I also run London AI × DNA, a research community across KCL, UCL, Imperial and the Francis Crick Institute, and write Scripts to Sequences — notes on AI and biology, from papers to small projects.

Selected work

GI × Proto — turning a genomic model into a sequence designer

Genomic Intelligence Builder Challenge, 2026 · 1st place

A closed-loop system pairing a genomic foundation model with an optimiser to design DNA for expression and cell-type specificity. Across 50 human genes and 1,500 enhancers, predicted expression improved in 49/50 genes, and enhancer specificity could be steered between HepG2 and K562 cells.

Shortening regulatory DNA without losing function

King's College London, 2024 – present

Used DeepMind's AlphaGenome to find and optimise the regulatory elements driving stable gene expression across human cell types — cutting sequence length while preserving function. Alongside it, LLMs that predict gene expression in yeast from pre-trained genomic language model embeddings.

CLAM — mining 1,011 yeast genomes for antimicrobial peptides

University College London, 2023 – 2024

A Python pipeline that simulates in silico peptide cleavage and predicts antimicrobial activity, screening 1,011 S. cerevisiae genomes for novel AMPs. Fine-tuned ESM2 for multi-label prediction across 22 functional classes — 99.2% accuracy for anti-MRSA and 98.9% for antibiofilm, beating existing classifiers.

InFinity 1.0 — open-source affinity maturation

iGEM, 2022

An open-source tool, built in three weeks, for increasing protein affinity to a chosen ligand at high throughput — published as part of an open framework for exploring mutational landscapes in silico. I also designed and built the team wiki, nominated Best Wiki out of 200+ teams.

cortiSense — a wearable cortisol biosensor

Nutrix, 2021 – 2024

Fabricated the gold sensing components by lithography and microfabrication. Separately, collected and analysed 1,500+ time-series glucose datasets and supported ML model development for glucose prediction in Python.

Living electrode-on-a-chip

Imperial College London, MRes (Distinction), 2022 – 2023

A microfluidic, microphysiological model for studying bioelectronic neural interfaces in vitro with iPSC-derived neurons — including hydrogel scaffolds for stem cell development and confocal imaging of neuronal differentiation and innervation.
