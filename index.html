Selected work

GI × Proto — turning a genomic model into a sequence designer

1st place

Genomic Intelligence Builder Challenge · 2026

A closed-loop AI system that pairs a genomic foundation model with an optimiser to design DNA for expression and cell-type specificity. Tested across 50 human genes and 1,500 enhancers: predicted expression improved in 49/50 genes, and enhancer specificity could be steered between HepG2 and K562 cell types.

  • genomic LMs
  • sequence design
  • closed-loop optimisation

Shortening regulatory DNA without losing function

King's College London · 2024–present

Applied DeepMind's AlphaGenome to find and optimise the regulatory elements that drive stable gene expression across human cell types — cutting sequence length while preserving function. In parallel, trained LLMs that predict gene expression in yeast from pre-trained genomic language model embeddings.

  • AlphaGenome
  • regulatory elements
  • expression prediction

CLAM — mining 1,011 yeast genomes for antimicrobial peptides

University College London · 2023–2024

A Python pipeline that simulates in silico peptide cleavage and predicts antimicrobial activity, screening 1,011 S. cerevisiae genomes for novel AMPs. Fine-tuned ESM2 for multi-label activity prediction across 22 functional classes — 99.2% accuracy for anti-MRSA and 98.9% for antibiofilm activity, beating the existing classifiers.

  • ESM2
  • protein language models
  • pipeline

InFinity 1.0 — open-source affinity maturation

iGEM · 2022

An open-source tool, built in three weeks, for increasing protein affinity to a chosen ligand at high throughput. Part of the work published as an open framework for exploring mutational landscapes in silico. I also led the design and build of the team wiki (HTML/CSS/JS), nominated Best Wiki out of 200+ teams.

  • open source
  • protein engineering
  • web

cortiSense — a wearable cortisol biosensor

Nutrix · 2021–2024

Fabricated gold sensing components by lithography and microfabrication for a cortisol biosensor. Separately, collected and analysed 1,500+ time-series glucose datasets and supported ML model development for glucose prediction in Python.

  • microfabrication
  • time-series ML
  • hardware

Living electrode-on-a-chip

Imperial College London · MRes, Distinction

A microfluidic, microphysiological model for studying bioelectronic neural interfaces in vitro with iPSC-derived neurons — including hydrogel scaffolds for stem cell development and confocal imaging of neuronal differentiation and innervation.

  • microfluidics
  • iPSC neurons
  • confocal
